# Gameplay Guide (for teams and the audience)

## The game in one minute
- Each team gets the same **purse: $25M** of auction credits.
- Candidates come up one at a time under a **tweaked name** with clues about their profile. Their real name, offer letter and salary stay secret until the end.
- Teams **call out bids**; the auctioneer enters them. The opening bid is the **base price ($1M)**; each new bid must be higher.
- When the hammer falls the candidate is **SOLD** to the highest bidder and the price leaves that team's purse.
- Every team must buy **at least 3** candidates (and at most 9). Teams with fewer than 3 at the end are **disqualified**.
- **How you win:** after bidding, the **Final Reveal** shows each purchased profile's real name, actual offer letter and **annual salary**. The team whose profiles add up to the **highest combined annual offer amount** wins — not the team with the most profiles, and the credits you spent are not subtracted. Example: 3 profiles worth $431,000 beat 4 profiles worth $400,000. A tie goes to the team with more credits left; if still tied, both win.

## Your max bid
You can never bid money you will need for the rest of your minimum squad. The screen shows each team's **Max bid**:

> max bid = money left − $1M for every other candidate you still must buy

| Your squad | Money left | Max bid |
|---|---|---|
| 0 bought | $25M | **$23M** (keeps $1M each for candidates 2 and 3) |
| 1 bought for $2M | $23M | **$22M** |
| 2 bought | any | = money left |

## Reading the live screen
- **Top:** ON AIR / Paused / Final, event name, Round · Pool (candidates still waiting) · Sold.
- **Left:** the candidate on the block — number, role, name, base price, **current bid** (and how many times the base it is), the team **bidding now**, and the full description.
- **Right:** every team's max bid, money left (purse) and squad size. The team holding the highest bid jumps to the top.
- **Bottom ticker:** live bid, last sale, top sale, top spender, totals and announcements.
- **SOLD!** banner names the buying team and the price.
- After bidding: **"Final reveal coming up"** with the final squads, then the **Final Reveal** — for each purchased profile the tweaked name, the original candidate, the actual offer letter, the annual salary and the buying team. Each salary is added to the team's **combined annual offers** on the right.
- The **final leaderboard**: Team · Profiles purchased · Combined annual offer amount · Rank, with the winner highlighted.

## Tips
Read the clues: the winner is decided by the real salaries behind the profiles, so one strong profile can be worth more than two average ones. Plan your three must-buys early: overspending on one star can leave you short of credits for the minimum squad — and a disqualified team cannot win. Unsold candidates may come back in a later round.
